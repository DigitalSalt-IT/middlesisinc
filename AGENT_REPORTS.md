# 👥 How to Generate Individual Agent Reports

## Quick Guide to Operations Reports by Employee

### For Julia Matheos

1. **Open Dashboard**
   - Double-click `index.html`

2. **Upload Data**
   - Drag your commission Excel file

3. **Filter for Julia**
   - In "Filter by Agent" dropdown
   - Select "Julia Matheos"

4. **View Her Performance**
   - See her performance card at top
   - Table shows only her shipments

5. **Print or Export**
   - Click "Print Report" for PDF
   - Or click "Export CSV" for Excel

### For Nicole Caporusso

Same steps, just select "Nicole Caporusso" in the filter.

---

## What Each Agent Report Shows

### Performance Card Includes:
- ✅ Total shipments handled
- ✅ Total revenue generated
- ✅ Total carrier costs
- ✅ Total profit made
- ✅ Average profit margin %
- ✅ Average commission per shipment
- ✅ **Total commission owed** (big number)

### Detail Table Shows:
- Every shipment they handled
- Date, order number, customer
- Carrier used
- Revenue, cost, profit
- Individual commission amount

---

## Use Cases

### Weekly Reviews
- Filter by agent
- Review their performance
- Discuss high/low margin shipments

### Monthly Payroll
- Filter by agent
- See pending commissions
- Export CSV for records
- Print for employee

### Performance Comparisons
- Don't filter (show all)
- See all agents side-by-side
- Compare revenue, profit, commission
- Identify top performers

---

## Printing Individual Reports

### Clean Report for Employee:

1. Upload file
2. Filter by their name
3. Click "Print Report"
4. In print dialog:
   - Choose "Save as PDF"
   - Or select printer
5. Give to employee

**What prints:**
- Their performance card (stats)
- Complete table of their shipments
- Clean, professional format
- No upload buttons or filters

---

## Tracking Over Time

### Compare Month to Month:

1. Export January commissions from Logistically
2. Upload to dashboard
3. Print Julia's report → save as "Julia_January.pdf"
4. Clear dashboard
5. Export February commissions
6. Upload to dashboard
7. Print Julia's report → save as "Julia_February.pdf"
8. Compare the two PDFs
